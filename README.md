## 🛠 ***Setup & Run***

### 1️⃣ ***Open the Project***

1.  Open ***Downloads*** on your Mac
2.  Double-click the ZIP file to extract it
3.  Open ***Flappy bird.xcodeproj***

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 2️⃣ ***Select Target & Signing***

1.  In Xcode, click the ***folder icon*** near the close button
2.  Click ***Flappy Bird*** (***the one with NO folder icon***)
3.  Make sure ***TARGETS***

On the right panel:

-   Open ***Signing & Capabilities***
-   Enable ***Automatically manage signing***
-   Click ***Team***
    -   Select your Apple ID if already added
    -   Or click ***Add an account...***
        -   Choose ***Apple ID***
        -   Sign in with your Apple ID and password
        -   If you don't have an Apple ID, create one in
            ***System Settings → Sign in to Apple***

***Any team type works***, including ***Personal Team***.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 3️⃣ ***Change Bundle Identifier***

⚠️ ***You must change the Bundle Identifier.***

#### ***If you own a domain***

Format:

    com.yourdomain.flappy-bird

Example:

    com.google.flappy-bird

Optional subdomains:

    com.yourdomain.game-dev.flappy-bird

#### ***If you don't own a domain***

Use:

    com.yourname.flappy-bird

If `flappy-bird` does not work, replace it with ***any name***
(example: `among-us`).

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4️⃣ ***Connect iPhone***

1.  Connect your iPhone to your Mac with the charging cable
2.  If charging starts → ***good sign***
3.  When asked ***"Trust This Mac?"***
    -   Tap ***Yes***
    -   Enter your passcode

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 5️⃣ ***Select Device in Xcode***

1.  At the top of Xcode, open the ***device dropdown***
2.  Select your ***iPhone***
3.  Click the ***Run (▶)*** button
4.  If prompted, ***unlock your iPhone***

------------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6️⃣ ***Play***

-   The game should open automatically
-   If it doesn't:
    -   Look for ***Flappy Bird*** on your iPhone
    -   Tap to open

🎉 ***You can now disconnect your iPhone***

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## ❌ ***Troubleshooting***

### ***Build Succeeded but App Didn't Open***

-   Find the app manually on your iPhone
-   If it opens → ***everything is working***

### ***Build Failed***

Possible reasons:

-   Project is ***uses old api so runs on older ios versions*** and some code no longer works
-   iPhone was ***unplugged*** during build
-   ***Signing*** or ***Bundle Identifier*** is incorrect
